Luggage Guide

Check baggage allowance and restrictions.

Carry-On

Weight: 7 kg (15 lbs)

Size: 56 x 36 x 23 cm

Included with all tickets

Checked Baggage

Weight: 20-30 kg (depending on class)

Size: Max 158 cm total dimensions

Extra fee for excess weight

Prohibited Items
  • Flammable liquids
  • Sharp objects
  • Compressed gases
  • Explosives
Tips
  • Label your bags inside and out
  • Pack liquids in sealed bags
  • Remove power banks from checked luggage
